Termites

Subterranean termites eat through timber framing unseen, often for years before any damage shows.

Warning signs

  • Mud tubes along brickwork, weep holes or foundations
  • Hollow-sounding skirting boards and door frames
  • Doors and windows that suddenly stick

How we treat it

A thorough inspection to AS 3660.2, then a chemical soil barrier or baiting system to protect the whole home.
