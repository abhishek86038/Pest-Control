Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers regarding pet safety, preparation, warranties, and weather.

Are your pest treatments safe for dogs, cats, and young children? ▼
Yes, 100%. We utilize APVMA-registered, synthetic pyrethroid and micro-encapsulated formulations that bond to surfaces once dry. Once the treatment has dried (typically 1–2 hours), there are zero airborne residues or toxic transfer hazards for pets or children.
Do we need to empty our kitchen cupboards before general treatment? ▼
In almost all cases, no! Unlike outdated spray practices, we utilize targeted gel baits and micro-droplet perimeter applications along hinges, baseboards, and harborages. There is no need to unpack food cupboards unless there is an extreme active German cockroach infestation.
What happens if it rains immediately after an exterior perimeter spray? ▼
Once our exterior treatments have had 20–30 minutes to bind to brick, concrete, and soil, rain does not wash them away. However, if an unexpected downpour occurs during application, our 100% Money-Back Guarantee covers a completely free return re-treatment.
How does the 100% Money-Back Guarantee work if pests return? ▼
If you observe covered pests inside your property during the warranty window, simply call us. We will dispatch a senior technician to retreat the problem area at zero charge. If you are still unsatisfied, we provide a full refund of your service fee.
How often should an Australian home undergo a termite inspection? ▼
Australian Standard AS 3660.2 mandates a comprehensive visual and thermal termite inspection at least once every 12 months (or every 6 months in high-risk zones like South East Queensland). Termite damage is generally not covered by home insurance policies, making annual checks essential.
